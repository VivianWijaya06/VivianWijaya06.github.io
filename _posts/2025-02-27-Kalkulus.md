# Week 3
## 2025-03-13
Pada pertemuan di minggu ini, saya telah mempelajari mengenai aturan aturan pada turunan:<br>
• Constant Rule: f(x) = c then f′(x) = 0 <br>
• Constant Multiple Rule: g(x) = c·f(x) then g′(x) = c·f′(x) <br>
• Power Rule: f(x) = x^n then f′(x) = nx^(n−1) <br>
• SumandDifference Rule: h(x) = f(x)±g(x) thenh′(x) = f′(x)±g′(x) <br>
• Product Rule: h(x) = f(x)g(x) then h′(x) = f′(x)g(x) +f(x)g′(x) <br>
• Quotient Rule: h(x) = f(x)/g(x) then h′(x) = f′(x)g(x)−f(x)g′(x)/(g(x))^2 <br>
• Chain Rule: h(x) = f(g(x)) then h′(x) = f′(g(x))g′(x) <br>

• Turunan trigonometri:<br>
– f(x) =sin(x) then f′(x) = cos(x)<br>
– f(x) =cos(x) then f′(x) = −sin(x)<br>
– f(x) =tan(x) then f′(x) = sec2(x)<br>
– f(x) =sec(x) then f′(x) = sec(x)tan(x)<br>
– f(x) =cot(x) then f′(x) = −csc2(x)<br>
– f(x) =csc(x) then f′(x) = −csc(x)cot(x) <br>

• Turunan eksponensial<br>
– f(x) =a^x then f′(x) = ln(a)a^x<br>
– f(x) =e^x then f′(x) = e^x<br>
– f(x) =a^g(x) then f′(x) = ln(a)a^(g(x))g′(x)<br>
– f(x) =e^g(x) then f′(x) = e^(g(x))g′(x) <br>

• Turunan logaritma<br>
– f(x) =loga(x) then f′(x) = 1/ln(a)x<br>
– f(x) =ln(x) then f′(x) = 1/x<br>
– f(x) =loga(g(x)) then f′(x) = g′(x)/ln(a)g(x)<br>
– f(x) =ln(g(x)) then f′(x) = g′(x)/g(x)<br>
